# It is inserted in final_generation_prompt.txt as {pose_details}; it stops the model from flipping the hand or the face.
Describe the pose of the person in this photo so it can be re-created exactly. Write 3 to 5 short sentences and use image directions (left / right / top / bottom of the image), not the person's point of view, except to name her left or right hand:
1. Which way the face points (toward the camera, toward the left or right edge, profile, three-quarter) and the head tilt.
2. Which hand holds the tool (the one nearest or farthest from the camera, her left or right hand) and where it is in the image.
3. Which side of that hand faces the camera (palm or back of the hand), where the fingers and the thumb are on the tool.
4. The angle of the tool and where it touches the hair.
5. What the other hand does.
Do not describe clothing, colours, skin, hair colour or identity.
