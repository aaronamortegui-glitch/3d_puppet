How to use:

1. Plug in and select one of 5 heat settings (lower for fine/damaged hair, higher for coarse).
2. Choose rotation speed: slow while learning, fast once comfortable.
3. Take a small section of dry hair; open the clamp lever and place the section (ends or mid-length) against the barrel, close the clamp.
4. Hold the iron vertically (tip up or down) next to the head and press the directional button: the barrel rotates and wraps the section around itself.
5. Hold a few seconds, open the clamp and slide the iron out of the curl.
6. Press Home to reset the barrel before the next section; alternate direction toward/away from the face for a balanced look.

Source: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H6CS24D5 (buttons/functions); clamp handling steps are general curling-iron practice (not stated verbatim)

How to use:

1. Start on completely dry, detangled hair (it is not a dryer).
2. Choose heat: lower settings for fine/damaged hair, up to 430 F for coarse hair; press Ionic Mode on for sleek/anti-frizz or leave off for volume.

5. At mid-lengths/ends, roll the brush to wrap the hair 1-2 turns, hold a few seconds, then unroll/glide out for a bent end or soft curl.

How to use:

1. Start with clean, damp hair; use the Concentrator nozzle to rough-dry.
2. Switch to an Air Curler barrel (hold Attachment Release, twist off/on) and section hair with the creaseless clips.
3. Prep with mousse and heat protectant; hold the end of a section near the barrel - the Coanda airflow pulls the hair in and wraps it around the barrel; use the opposite-direction barrel on the other side of the head.
4. Hold until dry, lock the curl with the Cold Shot, then slide the barrel out downward.
5. Pin curls to cool and set, then finish with hairspray.
6. For blowouts use the 1 3/4" round brush (lift at root, roll through) or the paddle brush (smooth in one pass).

## Product cards v2 (2026-10-21)

These cards replace the product sheets as the reference for generation. The sheets and `tools/product_sheets.py` are kept. Build the cards with `tools/product_cards.py`; they are written to `samples/products/cards/` (gitignored). There is one card per product and colourway:
- `01_auto_curler`, `02_thermal_brush`, `03_style_switch`: one colourway each (Nano Blue). The Style/Switch kits, Straight & Wavy and Curly & Coily, share the same body, so they go on one card.
- `05_flat_iron_pink` (Pink Rush BNT131THP) and `05_flat_iron_blue` (Nano Blue BNT131T-N).

Each card exists in two versions: `<id>_card.png` is labelled for people, `<id>_card_clean.png` has no text and is the model input. It holds:
- the main view;
- 1 to 3 other real views;
- the generated hand-and-product reference;
- 3 detail crops (buttons and LEDs, barrel or plates, hinge or cord);
- on the labelled version only: sizes, and colour and material with hex swatches.

Rules:
- Real product photos only: packshots, product close-ups and crops of them.
- No people and no real hands, so that no person leaks into a generation that uses another model or avatar. The ad photos with a hand were left out for this reason.
- The only generated image is the hand reference.
- Pink Rush has fewer real views. The only other photo is an ad callout whose leader lines touch the iron, so it was left out.
- A third flat-iron colourway, Iced Blue (B0GS7CFJ6F), exists. We have no product photos of it yet, so there is no card for it.
